Leak Detection Systems Compared

At one end are basic standalone sensors, and at the other are comprehensive systems that cover the whole home. Point sensors are small, battery-powered devices placed near water heaters, washing machines, sinks, and other risk areas that sound an alarm when they detect water. Smart sensors connect to your WiFi network and send smartphone alerts when moisture is detected, even when you're away from home. A whole-home system watches the main water line for unusual flow and can shut the water off automatically once it detects a leak.

Where to Place Leak Detectors

For maximum protection, place leak detection sensors in these high-risk locations: near the water heater, behind the washing machine, under the kitchen sink, under bathroom sinks, near the dishwasher, near the refrigerator (if it has a water line), in the basement or near the sump pump, near HVAC condensate drain lines, and in crawl spaces or utility areas with plumbing.

When Professionals Look for Leaks

When leaks are suspected but not visible, professional leak detection services use advanced technology to locate hidden water intrusion. With a thermal imaging camera, we see temperature differences that point to moisture under floors and behind walls. Acoustic listening devices detect the sound of water flowing through hidden pipes. A moisture meter gives an exact reading of how much moisture a building material holds. These professional tools, combined with the experience of our IICRC-certified technicians, allow Revere Restoration to find and address hidden leaks before they cause major damage.
