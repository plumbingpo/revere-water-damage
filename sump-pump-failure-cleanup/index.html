Sump Pump Failure Cleanup Help in Revere

Sump pumps do a single task, yet they commonly quit right when that task matters. Groundwater rises in a big storm and pours into the pit, right as the electricity fails, the float jams or the inflow outruns the pump. Water rises over the rim of the pit and spreads across the basement floor. It keeps coming until a pump is running again.

This water is not clean. Having passed through the ground, it usually holds bacteria, silt and drain tile residue, which makes it Category 2 at best, or Category 3 once it has been standing or sewage has mixed in. For the job, this means soaked porous items such as drywall and carpet pad are usually discarded, and what stays gets washed and disinfected first, then dried.

First Five Minutes

What to Do Right Now

  1. Stay Out of the WaterNever wade into basement water before the power has been cut. The furnace, wall outlets or the cord of the pump itself could be in contact with it.
  2. Shut Off Basement PowerIf the breaker panel can be reached while you stand somewhere dry, switch off the circuits for the basement. A panel surrounded by water is a job for the power company.
  3. Look at the Pump From a DistanceLook to see whether the pump came unplugged, its outlet tripped or the float is pinned against the pit wall. Do not reach into the water.
  4. Treat the Water as ContaminatedChildren and pets stay on the upper floor, anyone who has to enter wears rubber boots and gloves, and hands get washed after contact with anything wet.
  5. Move Valuables and CallCarry dry valuables upstairs, photograph the water level and the pump, then call us. Waiting for it to recede without help is a mistake.
Our Process

What Happens After You Call

1

Pump Out the Basement

Our crew arrives with extractors and pumps, which means your dead sump is not needed. Standing water and silt are removed from the floor, the pit area and any low spots.

2

Remove Contaminated Materials

Wearing protective gear, we take out the carpet, pad, wet drywall and insulation that the groundwater touched, bag it and haul it away so clean framing and concrete are exposed.

3

Wash and Disinfect

The slab, wall framing and remaining contents are washed down and treated with an antimicrobial product. Drying comes afterward, because fans on a dirty basement would blow contaminants everywhere.

4

Dry and Confirm

We run drying equipment until our meters show normal moisture in the slab and the wood, which typically takes 3 to 5 days. Make sure a functioning pump is installed again by the time drying wraps up.

Common Causes

Why This Happens

  • A discharge line that is blocked, crushed or disconnected outside the house.
  • A float switch that is stuck, tangled or pinned against the basin wall.
  • A tripped outlet or breaker, or a plug knocked loose, leaving the pump without power.
  • Water returning down the discharge pipe because the check valve no longer seals.
  • A power outage during the storm with no battery backup on the pump.
Why Speed Matters

What Happens if You Wait

Drywall and Wall Insulation

Drywall and insulation wick dirty water upward from the slab, well above the visible water line. The lower part that got wet is cut away, not dried, on account of the contamination.

Basement Floor Coverings

Carpet and pad soaked by groundwater cannot be cleaned to a safe standard and are removed. Laminate and vinyl plank trap silty water underneath and usually come up too.

Stored Belongings

Contaminated water is absorbed fast by fabrics, books, cardboard and the legs of furniture. Within days mold shows up on absorbent things that were in it, and they are difficult to disinfect.

Wall Framing

Framing lumber at floor level takes on groundwater before anything else in the wall. Once the drywall is off, the plates and studs are washed, disinfected and dried.

Insurance

What About Insurance?

Sump pump losses are widely misunderstood when it comes to insurance. A standard homeowners policy usually excludes water that backs up or overflows from a sump, and flood insurance is a separate policy with its own rules. Often the only coverage comes from an added endorsement for water backup and sump overflow, which has limits of its own. Your declarations page will list it, or your agent can tell you. Our records of the failed pump, how high the water rose and what it damaged go into your claim file.

Cost

How the Quote Is Built

  • How deep the water got and how much of the basement it covered.
  • Whether it is Category 2 or Category 3 water, since that decides what gets thrown out and disinfected.
  • If there are finished walls and floors, the tear-out of drywall, trim and carpet.
